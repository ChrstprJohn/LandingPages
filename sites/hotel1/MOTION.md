# Motion and photography

DcdHotel uses the layout of [The Hotel template](https://www.framer.com/marketplace/templates/the-hotel/) by Damir Cosic. The typography, section order, booking link, and template attribution follow the original site. The wordmark, editorial copy, and photography were refreshed for DcdHotel. [Framer Community license](https://www.framer.com/legal/community-terms)

## Page motion

- Hero photos settle as the label, headline words, and actions fade in.
- Section headings and photos fade up as they enter view. Cards, menu rows, reviews, and related text reveal in short groups as they enter. Once revealed, section content stays visible.
- The hero copy fades as it scrolls out. Hero and feature images retain their scroll-linked parallax. The Home link appears first in the main navigation and opens the root route.
- With reduced motion enabled, content appears directly in its final state.
