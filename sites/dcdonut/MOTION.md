# Dunkly motion and browser checks

| Component | Reference behavior | Local implementation and check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Navigation drops from 40px above. Letters rise from 120px below with an 8° tilt. Donut rises from 420px below with a −35° tilt and 70% scale. Copy, action, sprinkles and badge arrive later. | GSAP entrance timeline. Reference reload samples resolve the order and approximate timing. Curves are fitted to the sampled frames. |
| Hero badge and sprinkles | The outer seal turns clockwise about 26° per second while the caption keeps its tilt. Four hero sprinkles drift up or down by 10px and turn by 12°. The four lower sprinkles hold their angles after entry. | Independent seal and sprinkle tweens. Live measurements confirmed the changing seal angle, the stationary caption, and the opposing sprinkle directions. |
| Ticker and donut | Ticker fades and slides in from 200px to the left while tilting to −4°. The ticker then loops left while the donut floats over it. | Separate GSAP entrance, CSS ticker loop and GSAP float. |
| Flavour panels | The next scalloped panel covers the previous sticky panel. Labels slide in, buttons scale up and descriptions rise from 40px below. The stars turn up to 45° as they shrink to 70%. | Sticky layout and ScrollTrigger. Compared forward scrolling, backward scrolling, settled panels, two samples of the star transforms and add-button feedback. |
| Box plans and FAQ | Cards rise and fade in once. The box-plan donuts pop in separately. Hover raises plans by 12px and FAQ cards by 6px. Both tilt and settle on pointer exit. | ScrollTrigger, staggered artwork and CSS hover. Homepage FAQ answers stay visible. |
| The Dunkly Way | The cream seal rotates once in about 22 seconds. The large donut rises in and floats separately. Each thumbnail rotates continuously at a different speed. The hovered row moves right by 14px and returns on pointer exit. | Verified the separate seal and donut transforms and the 14px round trip. The ScrollTrigger runs on the row contents to leave the hover transform available on the row. |
| Reviews | A sticky stack with 26px offsets on desktop, 24px on laptop and 18px on phone. The heading is sticky on desktop. Card artwork spins independently. | Native sticky positioning and a GSAP rotation timed from roughly 13° per second. |
| Shop and product pages | Shop cards lift by 6px and artwork turns 25° at 108% scale. Surviving cards slide to their new positions on filter changes. The shop add button reads “add +” and measures 81 × 40px on desktop. Product info slides in as a group from 60px to the right. | CSS hover, GSAP Flip and separate product ring, donut and information entrances. Verified 8 → 1 → 8 results, the add-button dimensions and the cart update. |
| Build a box | The three step chips rise from 40px below. Selecting a size changes the tray and its donut slots. The flavour cards lift 6px and the artwork turns 20° at 108% scale. The progress bar sticks 24px from the bottom while the flavour section is in view on desktop. | Staggered chips, Flip transitions, and separate card and artwork hover. Tested all three selections, 6/12/24 slots, the fill link and quantity changes. Phone progress and action stack. |
| Our story | Photos rotate and rise from 60px below while the paired text slides sideways. Timeline cards lift 10px on hover and scale to 103%. The circular stats scale in from 60%. | Section-specific ScrollTrigger entrances and hover layers. Checked the timeline against the reference at desktop size and the two-column stats at phone size. |
| Delivery | Timeline badges emerge with their copy. Zone cards lift 8px on hover. The first FAQ starts open, subsequent questions can stay open simultaneously, and each open icon turns 45°. | Verified hover displacement, several simultaneous open answers, collapse and keyboard expansion. The four timeline badges stack on phone. |
| Contact | Form rises from 70px below. The contact cards slide in from the right. Quick answers lift 8px on hover. | Checked matching entrance poses, quick-answer movement, topic selection and the local email draft flow. |
| Find a shop | The map tilts into view and each pin bounces 8px. Location cards lift 6px on hover. The counter text enters from the left while the menu board rotates in. | Checked the pin transforms, map art, shop-card hover and reveal persistence on scroll-back and re-entry. |
| Menu and cart | Menu unfolds beneath the navigation. Cart enters from the right and tracks quantities and subtotal. | Native dialog and reversible transitions. Menu and drawer close with Escape and the cart carries across local routes. |
| Footer | Signup, links and wordmark caption lift in. The donuts spin in, grow to 110% on hover, return to rest when the pointer leaves, and can be dragged over the wordmark. Four appear on desktop and three on phone. | Verified 170px → 187px → 170px on the first donut, and a drag of 93px horizontally and 48px vertically after the hover fix. |

Compared browser layouts at 1439px, 1024px, 794px and 400px. Checked overflow at 320px. Desktop zoom-out was approximated with 1600px, 1778px, 2000px and 2388px viewports at a matching aspect ratio.

The follow-up used paired 1280px views and checked the seven named routes at 400px. The 24-slot tray also received a 320px check. Rotation phases and spring easing were fitted from browser samples, so independent animations can be at different points in their cycles when the two pages are opened separately.

The donut images, story photos and webfonts are bundled from assets publicly served by the [Dunkly reference](https://dunkly.framer.website/). All internal destinations render locally, including all eight product detail pages. The contact form prepares an email draft. Cart and checkout state stay local and the newsletter shows its preview status after valid input.
