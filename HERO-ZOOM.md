# FloraCo. hero at different zoom levels

At desktop widths, keep the hero full width, with the logo aligned to the headline and button on the left. The bouquet should fill the right side at its natural proportions, with no faded right edge or visible seam. Keep the navigation readable above the flowers and leave room for the tops of the collection cards in the first screen on typical desktop displays.

Use hero-relative sizes for desktop typography and spacing so they stay visually consistent when the browser zooms out. Give text a readable minimum size. Let the “Explore services” button size itself to its label and arrow. Size the padding, gap, and arrow in `em` so they scale with the label, which should fit on one line.

When changing the hero, compare the same desktop browser window at 100%, 90%, 80%, and 67% zoom. Look for sudden jumps in text or button size, overlapping content, a wrapped button label, and horizontal scrolling. Check a narrow viewport too, where the navigation can wrap and the page should still fit.
